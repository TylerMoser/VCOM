# pixelwood_c: reuse existing pixelwood families

Reviewed 2026-10-28. All tree/shrub/stump types in [pixelwood_c.jpg](../../pixelwood_c.jpg) repeat pixelwood_a. No new VOX files are needed. This is intentional deduplication, not an unfinished folder.

[Collection index](../PIXELWOOD.md) | [Duplicate crop sheet](reference/duplicates.png) | [Machine-readable duplicate records](duplicates.json)

The approximately threefold enlarged JPEG shows the lime and olive round broadleaf tree, square shrub, and stump. Olive crowns at the top and left are partly clipped; their silhouette and palette match A. Flowers, water plants, mushrooms, bridge log, rocks and terrain are not additional tree types. Use the seven treatments from the links below.

- [PixelwoodA_RoundLime: all seven styles](../pixelwood_a/previews/PixelwoodA_RoundLime_family.png), [VOX catalogue](../pixelwood_a/FILES.md).
- [PixelwoodA_RoundOlive: all seven styles](../pixelwood_a/previews/PixelwoodA_RoundOlive_family.png), [VOX catalogue](../pixelwood_a/FILES.md).
- [PixelwoodA_SquareShrub: all seven styles](../pixelwood_a/previews/PixelwoodA_SquareShrub_family.png), [VOX catalogue](../pixelwood_a/FILES.md).
- [PixelwoodA_Stump: all seven styles](../pixelwood_a/previews/PixelwoodA_Stump_family.png), [VOX catalogue](../pixelwood_a/FILES.md).

The apparent tile pitch is about 48 displayed pixels; modest JPEG scaling/compression differences do not justify duplicate model families. Reuse dimensions and validation records from A. Preserve this index when adding future assets; create new files here only for genuinely new source forms.
