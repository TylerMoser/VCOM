Models made from [new_1.jpg](../../new_1.jpg). The final VOX files are directly in this folder; each collection's notes, previews and records are in its own subfolder. Another agent's models of the same image are here too (`New1_Codex_*`, their notes in `codex/`).

## Claude's models (2026-11-04)

- [Every element of new_1.jpg](elements/README.md) but its cottage: 24 families, 168 complete models, each in Base and all six variations, `New1_*`: the olive cone tree, the tall grass, the small yellow flower, the tiny sprout, the broad-leaved plant, the flower box's plant, the roof's flowering plant, the coral and blue flowers, the woodpile, the chopping stump with its axe, the four figures as static figures (the hero, the fairy, the crested creature, the crow) and the ground tiles (meadow, light meadow, sandy path, and the edges and corners where they meet). [All files](elements/FILES.md).
- [The cottage](buildings/README.md): `New1_Cottage`, 7 complete models, made on VCOM's tile grid at game scale (a closed door, MagicaVoxel Glass windows) as the earlier buildings were, and tested in a copy of the game. [Files](buildings/FILES.md).
- The code that makes them, and the checks: [output/new_images/](../new_images/README.md).
