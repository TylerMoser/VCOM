# poke_m: outdoor stairs

One short flight and one stacked double flight with a middle landing. Building stairs are not recreated.

All files live in the parent image output folder. Each family has Base and all six established variations. [Shared collection rules and QA](../../stairs/README.md).

## PokeM_StoneStairs

Six warm gray treads, pale stone curbs and end piers. Canvas: 40 × 48 × 48 voxels. Rise: 24 voxels; 6 visible treads. Source calibration: 16 displayed pixels/tile. Crop: [122, 648, 181, 694].

## PokeM_DoubleStoneStairs

Two six-step flights separated by a short landing, complete as one model. Canvas: 80 × 48 × 64 voxels. Rise: 48 voxels; 12 visible treads. Source calibration: 16 displayed pixels/tile. Crop: [633, 440, 695, 520].
