# poke_m: Codex waterfalls and cave entrances

Repeated low foamy lips at approximately (330,470), (411,608), (420,834) share one family. Reeds, pale shoreline corners and water texture alone do not form waterfalls.

[Shared collection rules, placement and validation](../../codex_waterfalls_caves/README.md). Complete VOX files are in the parent image folder. `Codex` in the family name distinguishes this batch from other authors.

## PokeM_Codex_FoamySpillway

Low foamy river lip repeated at about (411,608) and (420,834). Reeds and shore outlines are excluded.

Canvas: 64 × 40 × 32 voxels; 16 displayed pixels/tile. Source crop: [301, 452, 367, 487].
