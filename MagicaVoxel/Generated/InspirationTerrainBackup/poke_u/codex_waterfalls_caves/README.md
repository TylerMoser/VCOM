# poke_u: Codex waterfalls and cave entrances

Two narrow visible falling-water ribbons: upper behind the northern house and lower through an island notch. Other blue gaps are atmospheric background; no arbitrary curtains added there.

[Shared collection rules, placement and validation](../../codex_waterfalls_caves/README.md). Complete VOX files are in the parent image folder. `Codex` in the family name distinguishes this batch from other authors.

## PokeU_Codex_UpperRibbonFall

Thin blue falling-water ribbon behind the northern house; inlet and lower end are occluded/cropped. 64-voxel completed length is an explicit estimate.

Canvas: 32 × 40 × 80 voxels; 16 displayed pixels/tile. Source crop: [180, 0, 215, 48].

[Source](reference/PokeU_Codex_UpperRibbonFall.png) · [All styles](PokeU_Codex_UpperRibbonFall_variations.png) · [Cardinal and underside views](PokeU_Codex_UpperRibbonFall_geometry.png)

Water width: 16 voxels; interpreted vertical drop: 64; tiers: 1; free island fall: True.
