# poke_t: Codex waterfalls and cave entrances

Eastern small rounded mouth and larger irregular southern recess. Tiny dark marks by the western boulder and upper cliff are rock/shadow details and are excluded. Source black void beyond the southern recess is not modeled as black solid voxels.

[Shared collection rules, placement and validation](../../codex_waterfalls_caves/README.md). Complete VOX files are in the parent image folder. `Codex` in the family name distinguishes this batch from other authors.

## PokeT_Codex_AmberCaveEntrance

Eastern warm brown rounded cave mouth near the sign; real arch opening, faceted rock brow and short through-tunnel.

Canvas: 48 × 40 × 48 voxels; 16 displayed pixels/tile. Source crop: [912, 414, 962, 463].

Nominal mouth width/height: [24, 32] voxels. Through-passage depth: 40. Floor top: z=2. Hidden depth is inferred.
