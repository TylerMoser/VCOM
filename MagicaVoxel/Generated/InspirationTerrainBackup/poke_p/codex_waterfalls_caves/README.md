# poke_p: Codex waterfalls and cave entrances

Upper pointed rock mouth with a real passage. The model includes a compact rock surround, not the whole gully.

[Shared collection rules, placement and validation](../../codex_waterfalls_caves/README.md). Complete VOX files are in the parent image folder. `Codex` in the family name distinguishes this batch from other authors.

## PokeP_Codex_RockCaveEntrance

Small pointed pale-rock surround in the upper gully, with a real open tunnel and a recessed earthy floor. Unseen depth is inferred.

Canvas: 48 × 40 × 48 voxels; 16 displayed pixels/tile. Source crop: [269, 88, 321, 132].

[Source](reference/PokeP_Codex_RockCaveEntrance.png) · [All styles](PokeP_Codex_RockCaveEntrance_variations.png) · [Cardinal and underside views](PokeP_Codex_RockCaveEntrance_geometry.png)

Nominal mouth width/height: [20, 32] voxels. Through-passage depth: 40. Floor top: z=2. Hidden depth is inferred.
