# poke_a: Codex waterfalls and cave entrances

Short western and bridge-adjacent terrace waterfalls share one family. White outlines on level ponds are shoreline foam, not extra falls.

[Shared collection rules, placement and validation](../../codex_waterfalls_caves/README.md). Complete VOX files are in the parent image folder. `Codex` in the family name distinguishes this batch from other authors.

## PokeA_Codex_TerraceWaterfall

Repeated short bright-blue terrace falls; western and bridge-adjacent examples share this size. Small white shoreline corners are not separate falls.

Canvas: 64 × 40 × 48 voxels; 10.5 displayed pixels/tile. Source crop: [35, 209, 90, 259].

Water width: 48 voxels; interpreted vertical drop: 32; tiers: 1; free island fall: False.
