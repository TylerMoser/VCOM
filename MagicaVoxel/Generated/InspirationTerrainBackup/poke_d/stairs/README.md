# poke_d: outdoor stairs

Includes complete lower eastern terrace and its side stair, plus northern rough stone flight. Long retaining walls and the barred drain remain separate future assets.

All files live in the parent image output folder. Each family has Base and all six established variations. [Shared collection rules and QA](../../stairs/README.md).

## PokeD_RoughStoneStairs

Six broad rounded stone treads nested between rough rock shoulders. Canvas: 48 × 64 × 48 voxels. Rise: 32 voxels; 6 visible treads. Source calibration: 16 displayed pixels/tile. Crop: [650, 371, 729, 423].

## PokeD_StairLanding

Complete lower eastern cobbled terrace with masonry support, parapets and a side flight; hidden underside and rear are inferred. Canvas: 112 × 80 × 64 voxels. Rise: 32 voxels; 6 visible treads. Source calibration: 16 displayed pixels/tile. Crop: [571, 546, 678, 678].
