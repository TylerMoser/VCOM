# poke_d: Codex waterfalls and cave entrances

Northern upper fall, central wide low spillway and southwest narrower fall retained separately. The barred drain is masonry, not a cave mouth or a waterfall.

[Shared collection rules, placement and validation](../../codex_waterfalls_caves/README.md). Complete VOX files are in the parent image folder. `Codex` in the family name distinguishes this batch from other authors.

## PokeD_Codex_UpperWaterfall

Northern green-water fall with irregular pale crest and a curling foam foot. Inlet is clipped by the image; a short inlet tab is inferred.

Canvas: 96 × 40 × 64 voxels; 16 displayed pixels/tile. Source crop: [324, 0, 423, 72].

[Source](reference/PokeD_Codex_UpperWaterfall.png) · [All styles](PokeD_Codex_UpperWaterfall_variations.png) · [Cardinal and underside views](PokeD_Codex_UpperWaterfall_geometry.png)

Water width: 80 voxels; interpreted vertical drop: 48; tiers: 1; free island fall: False.
